The cause was that `outline` is drawn outside the row's box. The tree's scroll viewport clips anything outside its bounds, and neighbouring rows paint over the top and bottom edges. That explains all three screenshots.

The selected row now uses an inset ring instead:
- `outline outline-border` became `ring-1 ring-inset ring-border`.
- The focused state `group-focus-within/tree:outline-primary/40` became `group-focus-within/tree:ring-primary/40`.

The ring sits inside the row's box, so the viewport can't clip it. The lint check on the file came back clean.
